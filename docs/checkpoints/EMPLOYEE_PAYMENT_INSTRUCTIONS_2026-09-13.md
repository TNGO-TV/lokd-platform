## Built

- Payment instructions bind a single exported, independently approved employee payroll item to its exact positive ZAR net pay, calculation hash/run revision, officer, employer, engagement, payroll profile revision and currently verified destination snapshot. Server-derived amounts cannot be supplied or reduced by the browser. Current payroll policy and original payroll approver grants/limits remain mandatory. Partner-company and zero-net items do not enter this employee transfer route.
- Immutable instruction contents, request idempotency and one active instruction per payroll item/provider mode. Independent finance approval is required, with beneficiary and maker user/operator separation even if the maker also has a finance grant. Separate payment operator policies retain current MFA or the explicit owner exception.
- Pre-submission cancellation retains history and releases only the unsent instruction slot for fresh preparation. After a submission claim, cancellation and replacement dispatch are blocked permanently in this milestone, including after timeout, failed/reversed provider evidence or source expiry. Future recovery needs a separately controlled resolution, not an automatic retry.
- The Paystack test adapter refreshes the exact recipient before dispatch and transactionally rechecks source, destination, grants, amount and evidence. It durably claims the saved reference before its single POST /transfer. Provider errors or process failure never permit a second POST. Fixed provider URL, explicit test secret, ZAR/balance parameters, safe generated reference, no redirects and bounded timeout.
- Verification retrieves the same saved reference. Explicit transfer mode, amount, currency, integration and recipient identity must agree. Signed test webhooks and API evidence must match before a test confirmation is shown. A new signed event invalidates an older verification; database watermark guards catch webhook changes during verification. Duplicate deliveries are harmless. Reversal, failure, mismatched identity and contradictory evidence cannot silently turn into a fresh payment attempt.
- Reuses the existing configured Paystack test webhook with a transfer-only route after signature/domain validation. Enrolment and booking collection/recovery behavior remain intact; no new webhook registration or R350 checkout was created. Original signed payloads remain transient; stored transfer projections exclude full bank details and unrelated provider fields.
- Operations UI provides preparation, masked source review, finance approval, cancellation of unsent instructions, test submission and saved-reference verification. Client, Officer and ungranted Operations accounts receive no instruction evidence. A test confirmation never writes a paid payroll status, bank disbursement or payslip.
- Live instructions may be prepared against real verified live destinations, but the dispatch and verification command refuses live mode regardless of configured keys. Existing payroll/disbursement/payslip database locks were not removed or weakened. Supporting a live controlled pilot is a separate implementation and acceptance step.
