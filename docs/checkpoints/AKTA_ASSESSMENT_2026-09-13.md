# Akta suitability for LOKD — 13 September 2026

Owner requested research of https://www.akta.tech/ during the LOKD payment-workflow task. This assessment concerns LOKD. No vendor contact, account creation, purchase, integration or upload of project/customer footage occurred.

## Recommendation

Defer Akta integration for LOKD's current milestone. Its documented specialism is broadcast and streaming video, while LOKD's immediate unfinished work is verified payment destinations, payroll/partner release, invoicing and recovery. The reviewed material provides no evidence that Akta would implement those functions or replace LOKD's identity, workforce eligibility or financial controls.

Potential future value exists for an officer training video library, live training sessions and searchable recorded video. These are proposed uses inferred from published capabilities, not tested integrations or accepted LOKD requirements. If a television/streaming project such as TNGO TV is being considered separately, the product category looks more directly relevant there; no assessment of that project's architecture or task dispatch was made.

## Information needed before a useful pilot

No usable public price schedule was found in the reviewed pages/searches; product pages direct visitors to a demo. Obtain a written quote covering minimum commitment, ingest/transcoding, stored video, delivery traffic, AI processing, seats, support and export costs before estimating value. No price or savings estimate is justified yet.

For a training-only pilot, first establish expected video hours, viewers and monthly watch time. Ask for API documentation, authentication integration, expiring playback access, event export for actual course completion, South African delivery measurements, deletion/export behavior and a small sample-based demonstration.

If the owner instead intends CCTV, body-camera or incident evidence, require demonstration of the actual ingest formats/devices, interrupted-upload recovery, original-file preservation, hashes/timestamps, audit history, controlled exports, retention/hold behavior and tenant separation. None was established by this public review. Generic object detection must not be presented as validated threat detection or identity proof.

Akta's public privacy policy describes international transfers, general security measures and purpose-based retention. It does not settle the hosting location or processing terms of a proposed LOKD deployment. Obtain the applicable service agreement, data-processing terms, subprocessors, region choices and treatment of uploaded footage/AI inputs before considering sensitive recordings. This is a procurement information gap, not a legal-compliance conclusion. [Privacy policy](https://www.akta.tech/privacy-policy/)

The public terms include broad service disclaimers. A negotiated enterprise availability/support and data-return commitment has not been reviewed; do not infer one from marketing reliability claims. [Terms](https://www.akta.tech/akta-terms-of-service/)

## Reusable decision

Keep the payment milestone independent of media procurement. Reassess Akta when LOKD has a concrete video use case and measured workload, using a limited sample and explicit acceptance criteria.
