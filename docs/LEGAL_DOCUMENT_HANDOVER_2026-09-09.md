# LOKD legal document needs — South Africa

Prepared for central collection after this implementation round. This is a proposed document list for Legal to assess, not a determination that every document is legally mandatory. No draft legal text is approved or published by this handover.

Priority documents before live commitments:

- Client master service agreement: contracting entity, authorized signatory, responsibilities and liability allocation.
- Versioned service order and SLA: exact site, work scope, risk plan, dates, price, service evidence, escalation and changes.
- Officer employment/engagement agreements for the approved workforce routes: identify the actual employer and distinguish direct employment from partner-supplied personnel.
- Payroll and banking authorizations/notices: wage calculations, deductions, beneficiary changes, records and disputes; separate partner-company payment terms where applicable.
- Privacy notice and identity/registration-verification notices and consents: Clerk, iiDENTIFii, PSiRA evidence, purpose, access, retention and correction; address identity matching separately from registration lookup.
- Payment, cancellation, refund and dispute terms: advance funding, cancellations, failed collections, reversals, service disputes and officer-payment responsibilities. Commercial terms need confirmation.
- Electronic-signature and signatory-authority provisions: evidence of the exact signed version, signer authority, timestamps, changes and withdrawal.

Other current needs:

- Website/platform terms, cookie notice and public enquiry consent.
- Officer and Operations conduct, confidentiality and incident-reporting commitments.
- Client site responsibilities, induction and safety protocols; specialist service addenda where relevant.
- Training enquiry/enrollment and approved-provider terms, keeping training separate from paid-work eligibility or any promise of placement.
- Data-processing agreements and provider/subprocessor review for identity, authentication, payments and hosting.

Questions for drafting: LOKD legal entity/registration details; approved employer/partner arrangements; authorized signatories; insurance/liability decisions; cancellation/refund rules; retention periods; training-provider commercial model.

Implementation placement: client agreement and exact service order precede collection/release; officer agreements precede activation. Current envelopes are watermarked synthetic rehearsals. Production signing, approved legal bodies and exact signed-order linkage remain unaccepted. No document should imply that PSiRA lookup alone confirms identity or permits deployment.
