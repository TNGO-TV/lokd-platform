# Officer pay basis

Migration `0193_officer_pay_basis.sql` adds `pay_basis` (`hourly` or `monthly`) to workforce engagements. Existing `direct_flex` engagements become hourly and `managed` engagements become monthly. Both remain direct LOKD employment. Operations can change pay basis without rewriting historical assignments, accepted agreements or payroll snapshots. Client and Officer views use “Officer”; pay-basis labels are internal.

Hourly payroll uses the higher of the agreed rate and the applicable monthly wage divided by 208 with a 15% premium, including a higher recorded lowest monthly salary in the same class. Each shift has at least four paid hours; Sunday and public-holiday shifts use double the hourly rate. These defaults and the 24-hour weekly review threshold are editable in payroll policy JSON. Weekly pay is the default; daily or monthly cadence records the Officer's request document reference. Operations can explicitly retain reviewed shift amounts instead of the hourly calculation; any difference and below-floor source amount are recorded as warnings.

PAYE, employee and employer UIF, SDL, PSSPF (7.5% each) and the employee NBCPSS levy (R9.40 monthly, apportioned to the policy frequency) apply to both pay bases. COIDA uses a recorded rate or raises a warning. Monthly service-pay calculation continues to use reviewed service remuneration. Deductions exceeding cash remuneration produce a recorded balance and warning rather than preventing calculation. Existing payroll approval and export authority remain enforced.

The following rules are advisory in application and database paths: monthly wage minimum, unresolved sector treatments, payroll-policy approval status, weekly hourly thresholds, booking/pay-basis routing and missing or expired employer compliance evidence. Warnings appear in Operations with an Override action retaining the original warning, actor, time and reason in immutable audit history. Any Officer can staff Bolt, Flex or Fixed; hourly pay on Fixed raises a review warning. Exceeding the threshold in three of the last four completed SAST weeks raises a review prompt without changing pay basis.

Officer identity and PSIRA verification, site readiness, client funding, actual agreement signatures, role authorisation, source versions, immutable history, environment and duplicate-submission checks remain enforced. Agreement acceptance records the hourly or monthly document classification and actual signed document reference; it does not generate legal wording.

The internal specialist cost floor uses the hourly basis for Bolt and Flex and the monthly basis for Fixed. Public price-book values are unchanged. Migration application and deployment are separate operations; preparing a release candidate does neither.
